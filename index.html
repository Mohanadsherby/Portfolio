Impact

What I deliver

Platform work is judged on release speed, reliability, security and cloud cost — here's where I move each one.

Faster, traceable releases

A standardised Jenkins → Nexus/JFrog → Argo CD pipeline, reused across services, that takes release lead time from days to minutes with full traceability.

Lower cloud spend

Right-sizing, Auto Scaling policies and storage lifecycle rules — driven by CloudWatch and Datadog analysis — that cut monthly AWS cost without hurting reliability.

Security as a gate

Trivy, SonarQube and Checkmarx wired in as blocking pipeline gates, plus AWS & Cloudflare WAF rules — cutting critical CVEs that ever reach production.

Zero configuration drift

Every deployment moved to GitOps with automated sync, self-healing and pruning — so environments stay identical and recover on their own.

Reliable operations

Primary responder for P1/P2 incidents — root-causing through logs and metrics on a Prometheus / Grafana / Datadog stack and improving MTTR.

Legacy brought into the fold

Legacy services automated with Ansible dynamic inventory, so monoliths and containerised workloads share one release and monitoring process.
